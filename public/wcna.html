Frequently Asked Questions (WCNA)

1. What is the full form of WCNA in Indian Education and Healthcare?
WCNA stands for Wellness Consultancy in Naturopathy and Ayurveda. It is the flagship medical wellness program created and certified by Educa Institute of Consultancy in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh.
2. Is WCNA certification worth it?
Yes! WCNA is designed for individuals seeking career opportunities as independent wellness consultants, clinical dieticians, or holistic practitioners without spending 5+ years in conventional MBBS/BAMS. You gain practical diagnosis, client consultation ethics, and treatment blueprint skills.
3. What is the duration and fee structure of the WCNA training?
WCNA is a 6-month intensive diploma covering 12 core handbooks, morning and weekend batches, practical clinics, and official certification. Affordable EMI and direct scholarship options are available through our admissions desk.
4. Can Arts, Science, or Commerce students join WCNA?
Yes, 10th pass, 12th pass (from Arts, Science, or Commerce streams), and college graduates are 100% eligible to enroll in WCNA at Educa Institute.
